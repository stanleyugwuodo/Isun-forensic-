Who we are

Building confidence through forensic science.

ISUN Forensic Laboratories is focused on the intersection of forensic laboratory practice, education and research. We aim to contribute to a stronger culture of scientific inquiry, professional competence and evidence-informed practice.

Our work is guided by a commitment to integrity, responsible scientific practice and the continuous development of forensic knowledge.

  • Laboratory-focused forensic services
  • Training and capacity development
  • Research and scientific learning
  • Professional, ethical approach
